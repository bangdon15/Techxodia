About Us

Company Background & History

Crafting complete, powerful digital systems so businesses never have to struggle with complicated technology.

🇵🇭 Philippine-Based Startup Technology Company Est. August 2026

Techxodia is a Philippine based startup technology company established in August 2026 and founded by Mr. Brandon Jake Torres and Mr. Jerem Villena.

The company was created with a simple idea: businesses should not have to struggle with complicated technology to achieve their goals. Inspired by the concept of bringing different pieces together to create one complete and powerful solution, Techxodia was built to help organizations turn ideas, challenges, and manual processes into practical digital systems.

The name Techxodia combines the concepts of technology and completion, representing the company’s belief that when the right digital tools, systems, and strategies work together, businesses are better positioned to succeed. This philosophy is reflected in the company’s tagline:

“
We Build. You Win.
The Techxodia Operating Philosophy

As a growing startup technology company, Techxodia focuses on developing customized solutions based on the specific needs of each client. Rather than offering a one-size-fits-all approach, the company works closely with businesses to understand their operations, identify opportunities for improvement, and build technology that supports efficiency, growth, and long-term success.
